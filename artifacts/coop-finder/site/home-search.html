Why a Custom Search Beats Zillow

Public listing sites are a starting point. A personalized MLS search is the real tool.

Live MLS Data

Zillow and Realtor.com can lag by days. Your custom search pulls directly from the MLS, so you see homes the moment they're listed — before they hit the public sites.

No Outdated Listings

Public sites are full of homes already under contract or sold. The MLS feed Tessa uses reflects current status in real time — no disappointment over homes that aren't actually available.

Matched to You, Not an Algorithm

Tessa builds the search around your actual criteria — not broad categories. She knows the nuances of each neighborhood and can filter in ways a public search tool never will.

Someone in Your Corner

When something interesting comes up, you have a local expert to call — not a national call center. Tessa knows these streets, these schools, and these communities firsthand.
