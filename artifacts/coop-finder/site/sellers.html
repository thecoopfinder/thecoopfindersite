Selling Your Home

Strategic pricing, targeted marketing, and dedicated representation for sellers across Newcastle, Tuttle, Blanchard, and the South OKC metro.

A Thoughtful Approach to Selling

Selling a home involves more than putting a sign in the yard. The right price, the right presentation, and the right strategy make a real difference in your final outcome.

Strategic Pricing

I provide a detailed Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) to establish a price that reflects current market conditions and attracts serious buyers without leaving money on the table.

Targeted Marketing

Your home gets exposure through the MLS, social media campaigns, my local buyer network, and professional presentation. The goal is to reach the right buyers, not just more buyers.

Strong Presentation

How a home looks in photos and in person directly affects buyer interest. I help sellers understand what to prioritize before listing to make the best possible first impression.

Expert Negotiation

From initial offers to inspection repair requests, I work to protect your equity and negotiate terms that align with your timeline and goals.

Transaction Management

Inspections, appraisals, title work, and lender coordination. I manage the process proactively so issues get addressed before they become problems.

Clear Communication

You'll know what's happening at every stage. I keep sellers informed, answer questions promptly, and make sure the path to closing stays on track.

The Selling Process

A straightforward overview of how we go from initial consultation to a successful closing.

1

Consultation & Valuation

We meet to discuss your goals, timeline, and the current state of the market. I'll provide a Comparative Market Analysis so you have an accurate picture of your home's value before we make any decisions.

2

Preparation & Pricing

We determine the right listing price and talk through any preparation steps such as staging, repairs, or improvements that will help your home show at its best.

3

List & Market

Your home goes live on the MLS with professional presentation and gets promoted through social media, my buyer network, and targeted local exposure.

4

Offers & Negotiation

I review all incoming offers with you, explain your options clearly, and negotiate on your behalf to secure the best possible terms.

5

Inspections, Title & Closing

I manage the transaction through inspection negotiations, appraisal, title work, and final walkthrough, keeping everything on schedule through to closing day.

Seller Questions

I provide a detailed Comparative Market Analysis (CMA) that reviews recent sales, active listings, and market trends in your specific area. The goal is a price that reflects current conditions and attracts serious buyers without leaving money on the table.
