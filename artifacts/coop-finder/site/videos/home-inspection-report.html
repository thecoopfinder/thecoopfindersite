Should You Worry About Every Item on a Home Inspection Report?

One of the biggest misconceptions about a home inspection is that a home either “passes” or “fails.” That is not how an inspection works. The report is there to help you understand a property’s overall condition before you decide how to move forward.

Inspectors document what they observe, from normal wear and tear to concerns that may need further evaluation. A longer report does not automatically mean there is a deal-breaking problem — it often means the inspector is being thorough.

The key is understanding which findings deserve a closer look and which are simply part of owning a home. With the right information and trusted guidance, buyers can use the inspection report to make a confident, informed decision.
