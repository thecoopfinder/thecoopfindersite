Should You Find a House First or Get Pre-Approved?

It's one of the most common questions I get from first-time buyers: "Do I find the house I love first, or talk to a lender first?" The answer is clear — get pre-approved before you start touring homes. Here's why it matters so much in today's market.

Pre-approval gives you a real, verified budget instead of a rough estimate. It tells sellers you're a serious buyer who has done the work. And in competitive situations — which are common in the Newcastle, Tuttle, and Blanchard area — a pre-approval letter can be the difference between your offer being considered or passed over entirely.

Shopping for homes without pre-approval is like going to the grocery store without knowing what you can spend. You might fall in love with something that's out of reach, or miss out on something perfect because you weren't ready to move. A lender conversation takes less than an hour and changes everything about how you search.
