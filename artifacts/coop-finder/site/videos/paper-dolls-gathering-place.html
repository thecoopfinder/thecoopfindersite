Paper Dolls Gathering Place – Newcastle, OK

Some of the best local places are the ones that make it easy to slow down, learn something new, and spend time with people who enjoy the same things you do. Paper Dolls Gathering Place has been doing exactly that in Newcastle for more than 20 years.

It is a creative place to scrapbook, make handmade cards, work on projects, and explore new hobbies. With specialty tools, supplies, project kits, classes, crops, and helpful guidance, the space welcomes both newcomers and longtime makers.

Marti and her mom, Sheryl, have created a warm community where people can preserve memories, build skills, and simply enjoy creating alongside one another. It is one of Newcastle’s true hidden gems.
