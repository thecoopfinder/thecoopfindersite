Do You Have to Sell Before You Buy a Home?

Buying another home while you still have one to sell can feel like a lot to coordinate. The good news is that with a plan in place early, the process can be much more manageable than it first appears.

Before you begin shopping, it helps to understand what your current home may be worth, connect with a lender, and look at how the proceeds from your sale could fit into your next purchase. Those conversations create a clearer picture of your options before you fall in love with the next house.

Every move is different, but preparation keeps you from feeling rushed or having to scramble after you find the home you want. If you are considering a move in the Oklahoma City metro or surrounding communities, I would be happy to help you make a game plan.
