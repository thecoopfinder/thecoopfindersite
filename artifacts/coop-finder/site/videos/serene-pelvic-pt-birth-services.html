Serene Pelvic PT & Birth Services – Tuttle, OK

One of the things that makes a community feel truly supported is having helpful resources close to home. Serene Pelvic Physical Therapy & Birth Services gives Tuttle-area individuals and families access to specialized care through many different stages of life.

From one-on-one pelvic health physical therapy and birth support to prenatal yoga, childbirth education, empowered pelvic push prep sessions, and Bowspring fitness classes, the studio brings several meaningful services together in one local place. Families can also find lactation support and infant physical therapy resources here.

Owner Alexis has built more than a service list — she is creating a community rooted in care, education, and encouragement. It is a valuable resource for Tuttle and the surrounding area to know about.
