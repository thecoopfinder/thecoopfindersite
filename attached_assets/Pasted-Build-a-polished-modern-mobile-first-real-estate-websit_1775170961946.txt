Build a polished, modern, mobile-first real estate website for an Oklahoma Realtor brand called:

Tessa Hood – The Coop Finder

Brokerage:
Knight Land Company

Primary focus areas:
Newcastle, Tuttle, and Blanchard (Tri-City area)

Secondary service areas:
Mustang, Moore, Norman, Yukon, and surrounding South Oklahoma City communities, while remaining available to help in other Oklahoma areas as appropriate

Brand personality:
Warm, approachable, trustworthy, local, polished, family-oriented, knowledgeable, and professional. The site should feel memorable and branded without being overly flashy. It should reflect a Realtor who is deeply connected to the community, especially for buyers, sellers, acreage properties, manufactured homes, first-time buyers, veterans, and individuals and households looking for their next “coop.”

Core branding direction:
Use a clean, high-end but welcoming aesthetic inspired by:

* soft whites
* warm neutrals
* muted blue tones
* touches of tan/gold
* subtle black accents
  The visual feel should be elevated but still personable and local, not corporate and not luxury-exclusive.

Important brand theme:
The “coop” concept is central to the brand. The site should subtly tie into the idea of helping people “find their coop and live their dream,” but the design must still clearly communicate REAL ESTATE. Avoid making it feel like a farm website. Use tasteful, subtle nods to the brand story rather than cartoonish farm elements.

Main goal of the website:
Generate leads for buyers and sellers, position Tessa as the trusted local expert, showcase community knowledge, and make it easy to connect through forms that will later integrate with GoHighLevel.

Compliance and content safety requirements:

* Keep all copy professional and compliant for real estate marketing
* Avoid any language that could violate Fair Housing principles
* Do not use exclusionary or discriminatory wording
* Keep community descriptions neutral and informational
* Add a disclaimer placeholder for brokerage and licensing language where needed

Do not use phrases like “perfect for,” “ideal for,” or “great for” when describing homes or communities in a way that could imply preference for a specific type of person or household. Keep all descriptions neutral and property-focused.

When describing communities, focus on objective features such as:

* location
* proximity to major highways or cities
* types of homes (new construction, acreage, etc.)
* general market trends
* local amenities like parks, shopping, and dining

Avoid language that suggests who should or should not live in the area.

Ensure testimonial content avoids references to protected classes or subjective statements about safety, crime, or demographics. Keep testimonials focused on the client experience, service, and transaction process.

Ensure brokerage name is clearly displayed and not less prominent than the agent name. Include a placeholder for Oklahoma real estate license information if required.

Include Equal Housing Opportunity language and/or icon in the footer to reflect compliance with Fair Housing laws.
